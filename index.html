Tylon Sohnle

Web developer & designer

I'm Tylon Sohnle, a web developer and designer based in Edmonton, Alberta. I design and build websites, web applications, and custom software, working on everything from the interfaces people interact with to the systems that power them behind the scenes.

My work ranges from building websites from scratch to improving existing ones, developing custom features, improving search engine optimization, and working with content management systems like WordPress.

Background

I graduated from the Web Design & Development program at the Northern Alberta Institute of Technology (2024–2026) with a 4.0 GPA. My work includes ParentCare.ca1, where I rebuilt an Edmonton non-profit's website, and developed a searchable resource library. I've also built independent projects like Vela2, an open-source desktop development environment, and Neptune3, a full-stack AI chatbot.

I'm open to development roles, freelance work, and contract projects. Whether it's something new or improvements to an existing website or application, I'm available to work locally in Edmonton or remotely.

Services

I can help with a new website, an existing site that needs attention, or a focused tool for your team.

Website design & developmentI design and build websites from scratch, redesign existing ones, and develop sites using content management systems such as WordPress and Joomla. My focus is on clean design, responsive layouts, and websites that are easy to use and manage.
Custom Applications & FeaturesI develop web applications, interactive tools, and custom functionality tailored to specific needs. This includes everything from search and filtering systems to databases, back-end development, and integrations with external services.
UI/UX DesignI design interfaces that make websites and applications easier to navigate and understand. From planning layouts and organizing content to creating wireframes and interactive prototypes, I focus on making the experience intuitive and accessible.
Website Optimization & SupportI improve existing websites through performance optimization, accessibility fixes, technical SEO, troubleshooting, and ongoing maintenance. I can also help with hosting, website migrations, updates, and adding new functionality as needs change.
